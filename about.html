Getting to know Thendral Electronics

Erode's dependable name in home appliance care.

Our story

Thendral Electronics was founded with a simple goal: to give the people of Erode a repair service they could genuinely rely on. Too often, appliance owners face unclear pricing, delayed visits, or temporary fixes that break down again within weeks. We set out to change that experience by combining technical skill with honesty and punctuality.

What began as a small local repair setup has grown into a trusted household name across Erode, serving families and businesses who need their AC, fridge, washing machine, TV, or RO purifier working the way it should, without the runaround.

Our mission

To deliver fast, reliable and affordable appliance repair services that restore comfort to every home we serve, backed by skilled technicians, genuine parts, and honest communication at every step.

Our vision

To become Erode's most trusted home appliance service provider, known not just for technical expertise but for the trust we build with every customer, one repair at a time.

What sets us apart
