Our services

Complete home appliance repair solutions in Erode. From cooling systems to kitchen essentials, we keep the appliances your household relies on running smoothly.

1. Air conditioner service & repair

Keep your home comfortable through every season with our comprehensive AC care.

What we offer
  • Split AC & window AC repair
  • Gas refilling and leak detection
  • Compressor repair and replacement
  • Deep cleaning and filter servicing
  • New installation and annual maintenance contracts
Common issues we fixInsufficient cooling, water leakage, unusual noise, foul smell, remote malfunction, and power fluctuation problems.

2. Refrigerator repair & maintenance

A fridge that isn't cooling properly can spoil more than just your food. We restore performance across all fridge types.

What we offer
  • Cooling and temperature control repair
  • Compressor and gas-related issues
  • Door seal and gasket replacement
  • Defrosting system repair
  • Water and ice dispenser troubleshooting
Brands & types servicedSingle door, double door, side-by-side, and multi-door refrigerators from all major manufacturers.

3. Washing machine repair

From delicate fabrics to heavy loads, your washing machine deserves expert care when something goes wrong.

What we offer
  • Front-load & top-load machine repair
  • Drainage and water inlet issues
  • Motor and drum repair
  • Spin cycle and timer malfunctions
  • Installation and demo service
Common issues we fixMachine not spinning, water not draining, excessive vibration, error codes, and unusual noises during operation.

4. Television repair & service

Enjoy uninterrupted entertainment with our reliable TV repair services, covering both older models and modern Smart TVs.

What we offer
  • LED, LCD & Smart TV repair
  • Screen and display issues
  • Sound and audio troubleshooting
  • Power supply and circuit board repair
  • Software glitches and connectivity problems
Common issues we fixBlack screen, flickering display, no sound, app crashes, and Wi-Fi connectivity failures on Smart TVs.

5. RO water purifier service

Safe drinking water is non-negotiable. Our RO servicing keeps your purification system running at its best.

What we offer
  • Filter and membrane replacement
  • Complete system cleaning and servicing
  • Leakage detection and repair
  • UV/UF component repair and annual plans
Common issues we fixSlow water flow, unusual taste or odor, leakage under the unit, and purifier not powering on.
